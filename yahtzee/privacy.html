Privacy Policy - Yahtzee Scorecard

Effective Date: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Marto Studios collects, uses, and protects information within the mobile application Yahtzee Scorecard.

1. Information We Collect

Our application does not request, collect, or store personally identifiable information such as emails, phone numbers, location, contacts, or sensitive user data.

Player names, profile pictures, scores, match history, and statistics are stored locally on the device for the app's functionality. Marto Studios does not receive or store this information on its own servers.

2. Third-Party Services

To improve the experience, serve advertisements, process purchases, and analyze the overall performance of the application, we use the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services: for core application functionality.
  • Google AdMob: for delivering advertisements.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: for detecting bugs, technical crashes, and improving app stability.
  • Mixpanel: for analyzing usage events and in-app behavior, such as button clicks, selected themes, game modes, premium purchases, ad interactions, and similar actions. This data is used solely to improve the user interface, stability, and features of the app. We do not use Mixpanel to collect personally identifiable data.
  • Google Play Billing: for securely processing in-app purchases, such as the Premium version.

3. Permissions and Internet Access

The application requires internet access to communicate with the aforementioned third-party services, including ads, crash reporting, usage analytics via Mixpanel, and in-app purchases.

The app may request access to photos or the camera solely if the user chooses to add or change a profile picture for a player. These images are used within the app and stored locally on the device.

We do not access your contacts, microphone, or location.

4. In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Marto Studios does not receive or store payment details, credit card numbers, or banking information.

5. Advertising

The application may display ads via Google AdMob. These ads may use device identifiers or other information provided by Google to serve, cap, and measure ads, in accordance with Google's policies.

6. Data Protection

Local app data remains on the user's device. We recommend keeping the operating system up to date and protecting the device with standard security measures.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach us through the app store or by emailing: contact@martostudio.com.
